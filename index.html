          Enrollment Guide

          What do I need before I start?

          Your personal details as written on school records, a mobile number and email you check, your home address, an emergency contact, and your intended program and year level.

          Will I lose my progress?

          No. Your entries save automatically in this browser, and you can use Save as Draft at any time. Drafts live on this device only, so use the same browser to continue, and avoid clearing site data.

          What is a Student ID?

          A 7-digit number such as 2500001. Leave it blank if you are a new student.

          The address list will not load or my barangay is missing

          Check your internet connection and refresh the page. If your barangay is still missing, contact the Registrar’s Office through the school website.

          How is my data handled?

          Personal data submitted through this portal is handled in line with applicable Data Privacy laws and institutional policies. Drafts are stored in this browser only.

          What happens after I submit?

          The Registrar’s Office reviews your registration. Check your email for updates, and keep your reference number for inquiries.
